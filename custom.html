    Work we have taken on

    What we take on

    New straps and hardware

    A strap that stretched, a buckle that bent, or hardware that finally rusted through. We replace it with solid brass or stainless and stitch it in properly.

    What we take on

    Stitching that gave out

    Seams unwinding, edges fraying, or leather that separated at a stress point. We open it up, restitch it, and finish the edge again.

    What we take on

    Upgrades and add ons

    Handle wraps to hold two straps as one, extender straps when a bag sits wrong, a closure that works better, or stamping it with your name.

    How it works

    Photos first, then a price

    Send the photos, including the close up of whatever failed. If it is not worth fixing, we will say so rather than take your money.

    How it works

    We reply with a plan

    What we would replace, what it costs, and how long it takes. Nothing moves until you say go.

    How it works

    Repaired and sent home

    We restitch, replace the hardware, finish the edges again, and send it back to you.
