    Ask about this piece

    Made to order. Most made to order pieces take three to five weeks from the day we start cutting.

    Made to your numbers

    Sized, not guessed

    Tell us what has to fit and we cut to that. Pocket depth, strap drop, belt length, the width of what slides into a sleeve.

    Leather

    You pick the hide
