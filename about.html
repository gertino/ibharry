    About iBharry

    We are a husband and wife duo making hand made leather goods out of Salt Lake City, Utah. One of us cuts and stitches, the other keeps the shop moving, and most evenings look the same around here.

    It started the way a lot of shops start, with something we wanted and could not find at a price that made sense. That turned into a few gifts for family, then a few requests from friends, then a bench full of tools and a stack of hides.

    We work slowly on purpose. Nothing is stamped out in a batch. If you order a wallet, the hide gets picked for that wallet, the edges get finished for that wallet, and the stitching gets done by hand on the bench in front of us.

    Living life in the process is not a slogan we put on a shirt. It is the reason we do this instead of something faster.

    What we make

    The bench list

    Wallets, card holders, passport covers, dopp kits, watch bands, tote bags, crossbody bags, luggage tags, and the small leather things that keep a bag organized.

    What we fix

    Repairs welcome
