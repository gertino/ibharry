    Included

    Every order

    Hand stitching, burnished edges, your choice of leather, and a proof of the design before we cut anything.

    Added cost

    Monograms and hardware

    Stamping runs from fifteen dollars. Solid brass or nickel hardware is quoted with your order. Rush work is available when the schedule allows.

    Payment

    Half down, half on finish

    We take a deposit when we start cutting and the balance when the piece is finished. Payments run through our Etsy shop or by invoice.
